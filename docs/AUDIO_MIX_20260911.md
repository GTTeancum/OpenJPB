## Confirmed mismatch

The shipped PC `playXA` at RVA 0x12BC00 does not use its legacy volume parameter. `setMusicVol` at 0x12BD70 forwards to `Mix_VolumeMusic`; the game entry loads options and sets this gain before gameplay. See the local decompiler exports for `win_audioStream.c` and the reconstructed `game_gPlayTheGame`.

Our audio adapter instead assigned each stream request's volume to its music gain. A request of 60 replaced the saved 27 (about 2.22 times the linear gain), or even replaced a chosen zero. This can mask positional SFX, whose channel volume remained 30 and whose distance/panning further attenuate them. It does not establish that masking was the sole cause of the user's reported missing laser sounds.

Corrected `pc_audio_start_resolved_music` to ignore the legacy argument, preserving `setMusicVol` ownership. Recreated audio adapters initialize from `OptionStruct.musicVolume`, matching the retail options-before-gameplay sequence. Stream-start logs now include applied mixer volume. SFX gain and attenuation remain canonical.

## Verification

- After correction, `--stream 5 --music-gain` tests the actual fighter boss track. Initial gain matches options; gains 0, 27, 30, and 75 survive starts with request 60, paused resumes with request 128, and deferred requests across the movie gate. All pass (`music-gain-final.log`).
- Actual fighter firing with a copied saved settings record initializes music at 27 and starts `dfrblstr` with SFX volume 30 (`mix-fighter-native.log`).
- The FED laser produces non-silent mixed PCM after a movie-gate cycle (`laser-pcm-final.log`).
- Release PC audio, sound, and audio-stream regressions pass (`*-mix.log`).

All evidence is under `out/live-fixes-20260908`. The mixer probe uses SDL dummy output and inspects native mixer state/PCM, without desktop input or physical audio playback. TODO #5 remains open for live audibility confirmation.
